# Hi, I'm Harish Saraf

**Founder of YUNOVA ([yunova.in](https://yunova.in))**: learn job skills and prepare for exams, free. Hindi + English.

YUNOVA is a free learning platform from India (launched 26 October 2026). It organises the best teachers on YouTube into clear, level-by-level learning paths, then adds topic tests, a final test and a verifiable certificate for skills.

- **Skills**: Excel, Typing & Data Entry, Tally, AutoCAD, Python, Canva, Spoken English and 200+ more: https://yunova.in/skills
- **Study**: free syllabus-ordered video paths for 60+ exams (CTET, REET, SSC CGL, RRB NTPC, CA Inter...): https://yunova.in/explore?kind=study
- **Certificates**: skill certificates can be checked by anyone at https://yunova.in/verify
- **How it works**: https://yunova.in/how-it-works

## The free promise
Learning on YUNOVA is free: no paywall and no trial. The site is supported by ads and by learners who [buy us a coffee](https://yunova.in/support). Videos stay on YouTube with full credit to their teachers.
